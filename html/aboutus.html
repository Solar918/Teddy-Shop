Our Story

Welcome to our cozy corner of the internet! Our journey started right here in Torbay, Auckland, with a simple mission: to bring a little extra comfort, warmth, and joy into the world, one hug at a time.

What began as a deep appreciation for well-crafted, timeless keepsakes quickly grew into a passion for sourcing and designing the perfect plush companions. We believe that a teddy bear is more than just a toy—it’s a keeper of secrets, a comfort during tough days, and a lifelong friend that grows right alongside you.

Why Choose Our Bears?

Every single bear in our collection is chosen with love and care. We focus on:

  • Unmatched Softness: We source premium, ultra-plush materials designed for maximum huggability.

  • Unique Personalities: From classic, timeless designs with elegant silk ribbons to whimsical, scruffy characters full of charm, we have a best friend for everyone.

  • Thoughtful Details: Whether it's a hand-knit scarf or perfectly detailed patchwork paws, we care about the little things that make a gift truly special.
